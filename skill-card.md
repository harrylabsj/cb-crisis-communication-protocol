## Description: <br>
Handle PR crises in any market with culturally appropriate response protocols, including press statements, social media responses, internal communications templates, and stakeholder updates localized for the target market. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Founders, PR teams, customer support leads, legal or compliance coordinators, and market managers use this skill to plan cross-border crisis communications. It produces structured crisis classification, stakeholder mapping, response templates, escalation workflow, monitoring plans, and post-crisis review guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Crisis communications can involve legal, regulatory, safety, privacy, or liability issues that the skill cannot professionally adjudicate. <br>
Mitigation: Use the output as strategic communication support only and have qualified local legal, regulatory, safety, or PR professionals review any real crisis response before publication. <br>
Risk: Localized cultural or market guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or based on assumptions supplied by the user. <br>
Mitigation: Validate local laws, platform policies, consumer expectations, and current market facts with reliable sources and local experts before acting. <br>
